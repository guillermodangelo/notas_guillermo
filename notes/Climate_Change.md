# Climate Change

**Summary**: Notes on climate impacts, natural hazards, and related datasets.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [Land surface temperature is not air temperature (LinkedIn post)](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7478690057026207745/): TC Chakraborty warns that satellite LST maps ("55°C in Madrid") exaggerate the heat people feel and overstate how much trees cool the air. He argues this misleads the public and erodes trust in heat science. See [[Remote_Sensing]] and [[Urban_Planning]]. *Keywords: land surface temperature, LST, extreme heat, urban heat, air temperature, trees*
- [Groundsource: A Dataset of Flood Events from News](https://zenodo.org/records/18647054): Open global dataset ([[Data]]) of 2.6 million historical flood events extracted from news articles in 150+ countries (Mayo et al., Google). Single Parquet file, CC BY 4.0. *Keywords: floods, natural hazards, dataset, news, parquet*
