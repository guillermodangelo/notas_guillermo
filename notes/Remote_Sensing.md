# Remote Sensing

**Summary**: Notes on satellite and aerial imagery, Earth observation data, and geospatial foundation models.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-25

---

- [TESSERA — A pixel-wise earth observation foundation model](https://geotessera.org/): Foundation model that produces per-pixel [[Embeddings]] from Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 time series. See also [[Deep_Learning]]. *Keywords: TESSERA, earth embeddings, Sentinel-1, Sentinel-2, foundation model, pixel-wise*
- [The Technical Debt of Earth Embedding Products](https://cloudnativegeo.org/blog/2026/02/the-technical-debt-of-earth-embedding-products/): Isaac Corley (Feb 2026) argues Earth [[Embeddings]] products lack shared distribution standards, causing glue code and high storage/egress costs. Compares Copernicus-Embed, Clay, Major TOM, Earth Index, AlphaEarth, Tessera and Presto. *Keywords: earth embeddings, geospatial foundation models, standards, cloud-native geo, storage costs*
