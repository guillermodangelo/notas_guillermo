# Agriculture

**Summary**: Notes on farming, crop monitoring, and agricultural field mapping.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [agribound](https://github.com/montimaj/agribound): [[Python]] toolkit that delineates agricultural field boundaries from [[Remote_Sensing]] imagery (Landsat, Sentinel-2, HLS, NAIP...). Runs [[Deep_Learning]] models (Delineate-Anything, Fields of The World, DINOv3, Prithvi) or clusters [[Embeddings]] (Google Satellite Embedding, TESSERA), with optional SAM 2 refinement. *Keywords: field boundaries, segmentation, Google Earth Engine, foundation models, Sentinel-2*
